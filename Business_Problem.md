# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behaviour & Retail Sales Analytics

## 🎯 Business Problem Statement

A retail company wants to better understand its customers’ shopping behaviour to identify purchasing patterns and improve sales, customer engagement, and customer loyalty.

The company wants to analyze customer demographics, product preferences, purchase behaviour, discounts, subscriptions, payment methods, shipping types, and customer segments.

The goal of this project is to answer the following business question:

> **How can customer shopping data be used to identify purchasing trends, understand customer behaviour, and support better sales and marketing decisions?**

## 📦 Project Deliverables

### 1. 🐍 Data Preparation & Transformation

Clean and transform the customer shopping dataset using Python to prepare the data for analysis.

### 2. 🗄️ SQL Data Analysis

Use PostgreSQL to analyze customer behaviour, revenue, products, discounts, subscriptions, customer segments, and purchasing patterns.

### 3. 📊 Dashboard & Visualization

Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to present important customer shopping patterns, sales metrics, and business insights.

### 4. 📑 Report & Presentation

Prepare a clear project report documenting the analysis, key findings, and business insights. Create a presentation to communicate the major findings and recommendations.

### 5. 💻 GitHub Repository

Maintain a well-structured GitHub repository containing the project documentation, dataset, data preparation work, SQL analysis, and Power BI dashboard files.
